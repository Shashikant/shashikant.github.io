**Context:** A QA team serving UK iGaming clients, with coordination between onsite (UK) and offshore (India) teams.

**My role:** Associate Project Manager, Quality Assurance (2023–2025), after progressing through QA Lead.

**What I put in place**
- Test strategy, quality gates and defect management protocols.
- Resource planning, timelines and performance metrics for the team.
- Root-cause analysis and data-driven QA reporting.
- Mentoring and Agile training for junior and offshore testers.

**Outcome:** Reduced defect leakage by about 25%.
