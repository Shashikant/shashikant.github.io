About

October 4, 2026

I’m a QA leader with 16+ years of experience, based in Pune, India. I started in manual testing and test management, grew into leading a 16-member QA team with direct UK client engagement, and today I’m focused on test automation architecture and AI-assisted quality engineering.

## What I do

- **Lead QA teams and delivery:** test strategy, planning, estimation, defect governance, and client-facing reporting.

- **Design automation frameworks:** Playwright + TypeScript + Cucumber (BDD), Page Object Model, data-driven testing, CI/CD with Jenkins.

- **Test complex domains:** iGaming platforms, including game-client testing on HTML5 canvas where standard DOM locators don’t work.

- **Apply AI to testing:** agentic test generation, self-healing, and intelligent failure analysis, plus test strategy for AI-based products (prompt injection, RBAC, API and security testing).
